# Privacy Policy for AI Resume Tailor

**Last Updated:** 16th December 2025

## 1. Overview
AI Resume Tailor ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data. **In short: Your data is processed securely on our servers but never stored.**

## 2. Data We Collect
We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our own servers. The extension processes data as follows:

-   **Resume Data**: Your master resume and generated resumes are stored locally in your browser's storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
-   **Job Descriptions**: We temporarily read the content of the active tab to extract job descriptions for tailoring purposes.
-   **Smart Fill Data**: When you use "Learning Mode," the extension saves your form answers locally (`chrome.storage.local`) to auto-fill future applications. This data never leaves your device.
-   **Form Data**: We may read form fields on job application pages to enable the "Smart Fill" feature.

## 3. Data Transmission
-   **Secure Cloud Processing**: All AI processing (resume tailoring, PDF generation) happens on our **secure backend servers**. The extension communicates exclusively with our API to perform these tasks.
-   **No Data Retention**: We do **not** store your resume or personal data on our servers. The data is processed in memory for the duration of the request and immediately discarded.
-   **License Verification**: If you purchase a premium license, the extension verifies your key against our configured backend or Gumroad API. This is the only instance where a specific data point (your license key) is verified externally for access control.
-   **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do not send your personal resume data to any third-party analytics, tracking services, or external cloud providers.

## 4. Permissions Usage
-   **ActiveTab**: Used to read the job description from the current page when you click the extension or trigger an analysis.
-   **Storage**: Used to save your resume settings and history locally on your device.
-   **Side Panel**: Used to display the extension's user interface side-by-side with the current web page.
-   **Host Permissions**: Used to communicate with the configured backend server for resume processing.
## 5. Your Choices
You have full control over your data. You can:
-   Delete your stored resume data at any time via the extension settings.
-   Your data is never sold or used for training purposes.
